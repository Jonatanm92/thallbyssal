# Market Validation Plan

Goal: validate whether Thallbyssal is worth pushing toward a paid release before building checkout, licensing, telemetry, cloud, or DRM.

## Target Beta Users

- Low-tuned metal guitarists
- Thall/djent/modern metal producers
- REAPER users who record DI guitars
- Guitarists frustrated by latency or overcomplicated amp sims
- Small creators making covers, demos, and original riffs

## 20-Tester Plan

1. Recruit 5 close testers who can give blunt feedback.
2. Recruit 5 guitarists who actively record DI.
3. Recruit 5 producers/mixers who understand plugin workflow.
4. Recruit 5 creator-type users who care about speed and inspiration.

Each tester gets:

- Private beta packet
- Known issues
- Feedback form
- Short test script
- Clear request for no public posting unless approved

## Weak Signal

- Testers say it is "cool" but do not record with it.
- Testers cannot tell what makes it different.
- Testers ask for unrelated features before liking the core sound.
- Few people complete the feedback form.
- Nobody asks for the next build.

## Strong Signal

- Testers record real riffs with it.
- Testers ask for more presets.
- Testers compare it favorably without being prompted.
- Testers report specific tone improvements instead of broad rejection.
- Testers share clips privately and ask if they can post them.

## Money Signal

- Testers ask when they can buy it.
- Testers offer to pay for beta access.
- Testers join a paid waitlist or preorder when available.
- Testers say a specific price feels fair.
- At least 5 of 20 testers would pay without extra convincing.

## Suggested Price Anchors

These are validation anchors, not final prices:

- Free private alpha
- Paid beta: $19 to $29
- Intro launch: $39 to $59
- Full price target: $79 to $99 if quality supports it

## Waitlist Copy Draft

Private draft, not public without founder approval:

## Beta Invite Copy Draft

Private draft, not public without founder approval:

> I am building Thallbyssal, an original amp sim for low-tuned modern metal guitar. I am looking for a small group of testers who can try the standalone/VST3 beta on dry DI tracks and give blunt feedback on tone, feel, CPU, presets, and DAW stability.

## Stop Criteria

- Core sound is rejected by most testers.
- Plugin fails to scan or crashes in common DAWs.
- CPU or latency makes tracking unrealistic.
- Feedback points to unclear positioning.
- Legal/branding risk is unresolved.

## Push Criteria

- At least 10 of 20 testers complete feedback.
- At least 5 testers record usable riffs.
- At least 5 testers ask for purchase details or next beta.
- Crash/scan failures are understood and fixable.
- Founder approves the sound direction and first public messaging.
