## Value Blocks

### Built For Low-Tuned Rhythm

Heavy high-gain rhythm tone is the starting point: tight chugs, clear pick attack, and low-mid weight that should stay focused when riffs are layered.

### Controlled High End

The tone direction is aggressive and powerful, but the top end should stay usable instead of taking over the mix.

### DI Calibration Direction

Input Match / DI Calibration is part of the product direction: helping players understand whether their DI is too weak, too hot, clipped, noisy, or ready before they judge the amp tone.

### Feedback-Led Beta

Early access is built around private notes from real players, original riffs, and practical recording conditions.

## Tester Expectations Section

Early access testers should be able to:

- Record or load a dry DI guitar track.
- Use original riffs only.
- Share private feedback on tone, feel, level, and stability.
- Avoid sending private sessions, client files, unreleased music, proprietary IRs, or personal data unless they intentionally choose to share them.
- Understand that beta builds may change or break.
- Avoid public posting unless a specific clip is approved later.

## FAQ Draft

### Is Thallbyssal released?

No. Thallbyssal is in private beta / early access. This copy does not approve public release.

### What kind of tone is it built for?

Modern low-tuned metal rhythm guitar: heavy high-gain, tight attack, thick low-mid weight, and controlled high end.

### What is Input Match / DI Calibration?

It is the product direction for making DI quality easier to understand before tone judgment. The goal is to identify whether an input is too weak, too hot, clipped, noisy, or ready. It should not modify DI files or guarantee a specific result.

### Do testers need to post public clips?

No. The default early access request is private feedback only.

### Can testers buy it now?

No. This early access draft does not include purchase access.

### What feedback is most useful?

Notes on pick attack, low-end tightness, high-end control, gate feel, output level, DI level behavior, and whether the rhythm tone feels useful in a real writing or recording context.

## Footer Safety Copy

Thallbyssal early access is private, manual, and feedback-led. Beta builds may change, break, or be unavailable between rounds. Do not use beta builds in critical sessions without backups.
