## Short Product Description

## Long Product Description

Thallbyssal is a modern low-tuned metal amp sim built for heavy high-gain rhythm guitar. It is aimed at players who want palm mutes to hit hard, fast picking to stay readable, and layered guitars to keep their shape in a dense mix.

The tone direction centers on tight attack, thick low-mid weight, and a powerful but controlled high end. The early access phase is not about broad public launch claims. It is about putting the amp in front of focused testers and creators who can try original riffs, record dry DI guitars, and tell us whether the feel, low end, top end, and output behavior are ready to keep pushing.

Input Match / DI Calibration is part of the product direction: helping players understand whether their DI level and noise floor are giving the amp a fair starting point. During private beta, that means asking better questions about input level, pickup output, clipping, noise, gate feel, and how the tone changes across different guitars.

Thallbyssal is currently private beta / early access only. There is no public release promise in this copy pack.

## Early Access Pitch

Thallbyssal is opening a small private early access round for low-tuned metal guitarists, producers, and creators who can give direct feedback on high-gain rhythm tone.

The focus is simple: does it feel tight under the pick, does the low-mid weight stay powerful without getting loose, does the high end cut without taking over, and does the tone sit quickly when you double-track original riffs?

If you record dry DI guitars and are comfortable sharing practical feedback, the private beta needs notes on:

- Pick attack and palm mute response.
- Low-end focus and low-mid weight.
- High-end control and harshness.
- Output level predictability.
- Gate feel and noise behavior.
- How your DI level affects the result.
- Whether the tone feels useful enough to keep testing.

This is beta software and feedback access, not a public release or purchase offer.
