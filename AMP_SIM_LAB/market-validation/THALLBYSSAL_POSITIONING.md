# Thallbyssal Positioning

Status: internal marketing draft for private beta / early access only.

This document defines the current copy direction for Thallbyssal. It does not approve public launch, paid access, checkout, licensing, analytics, telemetry, or release infrastructure.

## Positioning Statement

Thallbyssal is a modern low-tuned metal amp sim focused on heavy high-gain rhythm tones, tight attack, controlled high end, thick low-mid weight, and mix-ready response from calibrated DI guitars.

## One-Line Tagline

Modern low-tuned metal rhythm tones with tight attack, controlled aggression, and mix-ready weight.

## Short Product Description

Thallbyssal is an original high-gain amp sim for low-tuned modern metal guitarists who want tight chugs, clear pick attack, thick low-mid weight, and a powerful high end that stays controlled in a mix. The private beta is focused on rhythm tone feel, DI level consistency, and fast feedback from real players.

## Long Product Description

Thallbyssal is a modern low-tuned metal amp sim built around heavy high-gain rhythm tones. The goal is a sound that feels immediate under the pick, stays dense through palm mutes, and holds its shape when guitars are layered in a mix.

The current direction emphasizes tight attack, thick low-mid weight, and a powerful but controlled high end. Instead of relying on vague "more gain" promises, the early access focus is on whether the amp responds predictably to real DI tracks, whether the low end stays focused, and whether the top end has enough cut without turning brittle.

Input Match / DI Calibration is part of the product direction as a future workflow concept: help players understand whether their DI level is too weak, too hot, clipped, noisy, or ready before they judge the tone. For this copy pack, Input Match is described as a direction and early access feedback topic, not as a finished public feature.

The private beta / early access phase is for founder-approved testers and creators who can record original riffs, test dry DI guitars, and give direct feedback on feel, attack, noise behavior, output predictability, and mix fit. Thallbyssal is not a public release yet, and these drafts must stay clear of release readiness, purchase, checkout, compatibility, or performance guarantees.

## Core Audience

- Low-tuned metal guitarists recording dry DI guitars.
- Modern metal producers and mixers evaluating rhythm tone fit.
- Creators making original riffs, demos, and short tone clips.
- Players who care about tight palm mutes, fast pick response, and controlled high end.
- Beta testers who can give specific feedback instead of broad hype.

## Core Tone Pillars

- Heavy high-gain rhythm tone.
- Tight attack for palm mutes and fast picking.
- Powerful high end that stays controlled.
- Thick low-mid weight without loose boom.
- Mix-ready rhythm tones that layer without collapsing.
- Predictable input response through DI level awareness.
- Founder-led tone decisions based on real playing feedback.

## Differentiation Angles

- Designed around low-tuned rhythm work first.
- Frames DI quality as part of tone evaluation through Input Match / DI Calibration direction.
- Prioritizes feel and mix behavior over broad feature count.
- Keeps early access private, manual, and feedback-led.
- Uses original tone language instead of named comparisons.

## Public Claims Boundary

Allowed for public-facing drafts after founder review:

- "Original modern metal amp sim."
- "Built for low-tuned rhythm guitars."
- "Focused on tight attack, thick low-mid weight, and controlled high end."
- "Private beta / early access."
- "Input Match / DI Calibration direction" only when described as feedback-led or in development, not guaranteed.

Not allowed:

- Claims that Thallbyssal sounds like any named plugin, artist, amp, model, product, or brand.
- Competitor names in public-facing copy.
- Fake testimonials or implied user praise.
- Public release readiness.
- Purchase, checkout, pricing, revenue, or performance guarantees.
- Analytics, telemetry, auth, licensing, DRM, cloud sync, or launch infrastructure claims.
