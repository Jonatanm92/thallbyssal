# Creator DM Templates

Status: internal outreach drafts for founder-approved private beta / early access only.

These templates are written for manual outreach. They do not include automated messaging, analytics, tracking, checkout, public launch, or paid access systems.

## Outreach Rules

- Personalize each message with a real reason for contacting the creator.
- Do not claim the product sounds like any named artist, amp, plugin, model, product, or brand.
- Do not mention competitors in public-facing or creator-facing copy.
- Do not ask for public posting unless the founder approves it later.
- Do not imply commercial availability, guaranteed results, or release timing.
- Keep feedback requests specific and practical.

## Follow-Up If Interested

Thanks. The most helpful test is simple:

1. Use an original dry DI riff.
2. Try one or two rhythm tones.
3. Note the guitar, tuning, pickup type, and rough input level if you know it.
4. Tell me what feels tight, loose, harsh, muddy, too gated, or immediately useful.
5. Do not send private sessions, client files, unreleased music, or anything you do not want shared.

I will send the private beta notes when the next founder-approved build is ready.

## Follow-Up If They Ask What Makes It Different

The focus is low-tuned rhythm tone first: tight attack, thick low-mid pressure, controlled high end, and better awareness of the source DI before judging the amp tone.

Input Match / DI Calibration is part of the direction. The idea is to make it clearer when a DI is too weak, too hot, clipped, noisy, or already in a good range, because that changes how any high-gain tone feels.

## Follow-Up If They Ask About Posting Clips

For this private beta round, the default is no public posting unless the founder approves a specific clip later. The priority is honest private feedback on tone, feel, input behavior, and stability.

## Follow-Up If They Ask About Buying

There is no purchase link for this private beta. The current goal is to learn whether the sound direction is strong enough to keep developing before any public release or commercial system is approved.

## Feedback Prompt After Build Is Sent

After you have had time with the build, the most useful notes are:

- Did it scan and pass audio?
- Did the first rhythm tone feel tight enough to keep playing?
- Was the low end too loose, too thin, or in a usable range?
- Was the high end controlled, harsh, dull, or about right?
- Did pick attack and palm mutes feel responsive?
- Did input level or DI quality change the result in an obvious way?
- Would you use it again for another private beta test?
