# 30 Days Of Short-Form Content Ideas

Status: internal creative plan for Thallbyssal private beta / early access. These ideas are copy/planning only and do not approve public launch, checkout, analytics, paid access, or deployment work.

## Content Rules

- Use original riffs only.
- Avoid named artists, songs, albums, brands, amps, plugins, models, or competitor comparisons.
- Do not imply public release readiness.
- Do not use fake testimonials.
- Keep beta language honest: private, feedback-led, and still being shaped.
- Make the audio point clear in the caption: tight attack, low-mid weight, controlled high end, mix-ready rhythm, or DI calibration.

## Weekly Themes

Week 1: Establish the tone direction.

- Tight attack.
- Low-tuned rhythm.
- Heavy high-gain.
- Controlled high end.

Week 2: Show why DI quality matters.

- Input level.
- Noise floor.
- Hot versus weak input.
- Calibration as a feedback topic.

Week 3: Test mix behavior.

- Double-tracking.
- Soloed versus in mix.
- Output level consistency.
- Low-mid density.

Week 4: Invite practical beta feedback.

- Original riffs.
- Private testing.
- Specific tone notes.
- No public posting required unless approved.
