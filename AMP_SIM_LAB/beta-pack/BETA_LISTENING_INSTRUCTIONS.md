# Private Beta Listening Instructions

Use these instructions for internal/private beta listening of founder-approved Thallbyssal clips only. This is not a public launch page, not a release candidate, and not a claim of commercial availability. There is no public release yet.

Do not redistribute clips, links, notes, screenshots, rendered audio, or this page. Do not post clips publicly. Do not compare the clips to competitor products, artists, albums, songs, or trademarked amp models in packet-facing feedback.

## Before Listening

1. Confirm the clip link came from a founder-approved private channel.
2. Confirm the clip title, DI type, preset, and build/version are listed.
3. Use headphones or monitors you know well.
4. Keep playback level moderate at first, then adjust manually.
5. Listen to the whole clip once without writing notes.
6. Listen again for attack, low end, high end, and mix usefulness.

## What To Listen For

- Weight: whether the clip feels heavy enough for the riff.
- Attack: whether the pick response feels tight or sluggish.
- High end: whether the top is powerful or too fizzy.
- Low end: whether the bottom is controlled or muddy.
- Preset fit: which clip/preset feels strongest and weakest.
- Real use: whether you would use it in a real riff or mix.
- Input Match / DI Calibration: whether level/setup guidance would help.

## Manual Feedback Only

Use `BETA_FEEDBACK_QUESTIONS.md` for tone and OS/DAW questions. Send feedback only through founder-approved private channels.

This packet does not collect telemetry, analytics, crash data, system data, session data, audio, or personal data automatically. Do not send private sessions, client files, unreleased music, proprietary IR names, account IDs, license keys, or personal data unless you intentionally choose to share them.

## Bug Reports

If the clip has silence, obvious clipping, crackle, missing audio, wrong preset metadata, or other playback problems, use `FEEDBACK_FORM.md#bug-report-template` or the short template in `PRIVATE_BETA_LISTENING_PAGE.md#bug-report-link`.

Keep bug reports text-only by default.
