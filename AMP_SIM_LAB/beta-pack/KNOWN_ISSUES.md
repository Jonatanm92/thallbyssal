# Known Issues

- UI is temporary and not the final commercial design.
- Factory presets are internal placeholders and not public-release approved.
- Installer and uninstaller are placeholders until packaging is finalized.
- Supported OS/DAW list is a private beta placeholder, not a public compatibility claim.
- Offline render automation is not connected yet.
- VST3 manifest generation is disabled for development builds because of Windows path/build stability.
- No checkout, licensing server, DRM, telemetry, analytics, auth, or cloud sync exists.
- DAW coverage is not complete.
- Some DAWs may require manual plugin rescans or cache clearing.
- CPU use, gain staging, and preset output levels may change between private beta builds.
- Beta feedback should not include private sessions, client files, unreleased music, proprietary IRs, account IDs, license keys, or personal data unless intentionally shared by the tester.
- There is no public release yet; beta notes are not compatibility promises or launch claims.
- Private beta feedback is manual only; the packet does not collect usage, crash, system, audio, session, or personal data automatically.
- If the founder needs more detail later, the founder will manually ask through a private channel and testers can choose what to send.
